Data Sources
Built on Three Public Aviation Datasets
🛸NASA N-CMAPSS
Engine Physics
High-fidelity turbofan degradation simulation with 14 physical sensors across 6.5M flight records. Provides ground-truth RUL labels for model training.
✈️FAA Service Difficulty Reports
Maintenance Events
U.S. operator-reported mechanical problems and unscheduled maintenance — used to validate predictions against real-world failure patterns.
📊Bureau of Transportation Statistics
Flight Operations
U.S. flight schedules, delays, and cancellations — used to quantify the operational and financial impact of unplanned groundings.

About
Open-Source Research for U.S. Aviation Infrastructure

This study was launched in March 2026 by Arnav Prashant Joshi, a researcher specializing in data synthesis and optimization for complex operational systems. The goal is to close a long-standing gap: predictive engine maintenance has historically required costly enterprise systems, placing it out of reach for small regional carriers that are critical to the U.S. aviation network.

All code, pipelines, and model artifacts will be publicly released upon completion. The framework is designed to run on standard hardware — no specialized enterprise infrastructure required — ensuring data-driven maintenance is accessible across the entire U.S. aviation system.
